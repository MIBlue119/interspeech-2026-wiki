## Opus art-direction prompt

Wide 2.5:1 GitHub README hero banner, 1600×640. Crisp, flat, print-like editorial graphic design, calm and restrained like a frontier AI lab's research publication. Off-white #f7f7f2 background with generous negative space.

**Left 55%:** Strong left-aligned typography in a clean geometric sans (Geist-like), charcoal #20221f.
- Line one, large and bold: "Interspeech 2026"
- Line two, equally confident, in deep sage #557260: "Research Wiki"
- Below, smaller regular weight in charcoal: "Paper digests. Connected ideas. Ready for your agent."

Above the title, place a short, thin rust-orange #c94927 horizontal rule as the only warm accent. Render all text exactly and spelled correctly. No other words, letters, numbers, or logos anywhere.

**Right 45%:** A subtle, abstract speech motif. Several smooth, thin formant-like wave curves in charcoal and sage flow horizontally, layered at low contrast. They emerge from a quiet field of evenly spaced small dots, a dot matrix suggesting a collection of papers. A few dots are tinted pale sage #e3edce, and one or two tiny dots are rust-orange. The graphic stays secondary to the text and fades softly toward the edges.

Flat vector look with fine, consistent line weights. No gradients, shadows, noise texture, or 3D.

Avoid: charts, axes, numbers, statistics, UI buttons, browser frames, brains, robots, microphone clipart, purple or neon gradients, glossy effects.
