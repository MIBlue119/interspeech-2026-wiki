# Detection of Incorrect Place of Articulation in Polish Sibilants Using Convolutional Autoencoders

**TL;DR** — Multi-task convolutional autoencoder embeddings combined with SVMs effectively detect incorrect place of articulation in Polish sibilant sounds produced by children, supporting earlier diagnosis of a common speech disorder.

## Problem

Incorrect production of sibilant consonants is among the most common speech disorders in children, and effective treatment depends on early diagnosis, which requires reliable automated detection methods.

## Method

The authors combine convolutional autoencoders (classical, sparse, and multi-task variants) for feature extraction with support vector machines to detect incorrect place of articulation in Polish voiceless retroflex fricatives and affricates, using a corpus of 149-151 children aged 4-8.

## Results

The best-performing models use multi-task learning and achieve sensitivities of up to 84.32%, demonstrating that autoencoder-based embeddings are effective for feature extraction and classification in challenging pediatric speech data.
