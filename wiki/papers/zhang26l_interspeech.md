# CTMusic: A Traditional Chinese Instrumental Music Dataset Towards Text-to-Music Generation

**TL;DR** — The first annotated text-music dataset for traditional Chinese instrumental music (741 pairs, 42+ hours), used to fine-tune Stable Audio Open into a model that better captures traditional Chinese musical characteristics.

## Problem

Text-to-music generation has advanced rapidly, but almost entirely for Western music, leaving traditional Chinese music underexplored due to a scarcity of well-annotated text-music paired datasets.

## Method

Introduces CTMusic, a traditional Chinese instrumental music dataset with 741 carefully annotated text-music pairs and over 42 hours of audio spanning diverse Chinese instruments in solo and ensemble settings, then fine-tunes Stable Audio Open on it.

## Results

Fine-tuning on CTMusic produces an improved text-to-music model that better captures the unique characteristics of traditional Chinese music than the base model; the dataset is publicly released.
