# Beyond WER: Entity and Disfluency Recall in Accented Conversational ASR

[PDF](https://www.isca-archive.org/interspeech_2026/husain26_interspeech.pdf) · [ISCA page](https://www.isca-archive.org/interspeech_2026/husain26_interspeech.html) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.21437/Interspeech.2026-786)

**TL;DR** — A three-stage pipeline combining heuristic entity-rich data curation and regional LoRA adapters on Qwen2.5-Omni-3B achieves 80–85% entity recall and 76–86% filler recall while reducing WER to 6–10% on accented conversational English.

## Problem

Standard ASR systems optimized for low Word Error Rate (WER) routinely miss named entities and filled pauses in accented conversational speech, yielding entity recalls as low as 53% and filler recalls under 5%. This disconnect causes downstream failures in language-learning platforms that require both verbatim transcripts for fluency self-assessment and corrected transcripts for comprehension feedback.

## Method

The pipeline consists of three components: (1) SQL lexical heuristics operating on BigQuery logs to filter and retain entity-rich utterances (consecutive capitalized words, acronyms, honorifics, mid-sentence capitalization) at ~2.8x higher entity density than random sampling; (2) Gemini 2.5 Pro used as the automated reference transcriber for generating training and evaluation targets; and (3) regional rank-32 LoRA adapters fine-tuned on Qwen2.5-Omni-3B (3B parameters) using Unsloth and SFTTrainer for 2 epochs on 9k utterances per region. The model uses a dual-output format to output both a verbatim transcript (preserving fillers and disfluencies) and a corrected transcript in a single forward pass, alongside a boolean comprehensibility flag.

## Results

Evaluated on 6k test utterances across India, Indonesia, and Latin America against baselines including Parakeet TDT-CTC 110M, Whisper, AssemblyAI Universal-3-Pro, and a zero-shot Qwen3-Omni-30B. The fine-tuned Qwen model achieves 80–85% entity recall (up from 53–55% for Parakeet) and 76–86% filler recall (up from <5%), while cutting WER to 6–10% (a 53–60% relative reduction over Parakeet). Paired bootstrap tests (10,000 iterations) confirm that entity-heuristic curation alone provides a statistically significant 2.76–4.19 percentage point boost in entity recall over random sampling (p < 0.0001 across all regions).

## Applications

Engineers building language-learning applications and conversational dialogue systems for non-native or accented English speakers needing faithful fluency and entity-rich feedback.

## Limitations

Test-set references are silver-standard generated by Gemini 2.5 Pro rather than fully human-verified, a filler recall gap remains compared to larger 30-billion parameter models, and evaluation is restricted to English without addressing code-switching.
