# VoiceQualityGUI: A Tool for Word-Level Voice Quality Modifications

**TL;DR** — A GUI tool lets users tweak creakiness, breathiness, and nasality at the individual-word level in synthesized speech and hear how it shifts conversational meaning.

## Problem

Voice quality strongly shapes how synthesized speech is perceived pragmatically, but exploring this has lagged because no accessible tool let researchers manipulate voice quality at fine (word-level) granularity.

## Method

The authors build a GUI on top of an improved VoiceQualityVC voice conversion system that supports modifying creakiness, breathiness, and nasality down to the word level, letting users manipulate these properties directly and hear the resulting change.

## Results

As a demo/tool paper, the abstract reports no quantitative evaluation; it describes the working system for interactive, word-level voice quality exploration.
