# HASS: Hierarchical Simulation of Logopenic Aphasic Speech for Scalable PPA Detection

**TL;DR** — A clinically grounded framework that simulates logopenic primary progressive aphasia speech across semantic, phonological, and temporal deficit dimensions at varying severity, producing training data that improves PPA detection models.

## Problem

Building diagnostic models for primary progressive aphasia (PPA) is hampered by scarce clinical data, since collecting it at scale is limited by patient vulnerability and costly expert labeling, and prior speech-simulation approaches only model isolated dysfluencies rather than holistic PPA phenotypes.

## Method

HASS (Hierarchical Aphasic Speech Simulation) works with clinical experts to systematically identify and simulate the semantic, phonological, and temporal deficits characteristic of logopenic variant PPA at varying severity levels.

## Results

Models trained using HASS-simulated data achieve more accurate and generalizable logopenic PPA detection than prior simulation approaches; code is released.
