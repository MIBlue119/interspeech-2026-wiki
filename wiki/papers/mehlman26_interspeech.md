# Speech Entrainment in Multi-Party Conversations with a Digital Agent

**TL;DR** — In group conversations that include a digital agent, people still entrain locally to other humans, but entrainment toward the agent itself stays limited and depends on whether the group is adults or families.

## Problem

Speech entrainment — speakers adapting their style toward interlocutors — is well studied in dyadic human-human interactions, but how it plays out in multi-party groups that include a non-human digital agent was unclear.

## Method

The authors collect and analyze a new dataset of multi-party sessions with both adult and family (parent/children) groups interacting with a digital agent, then measure a range of knowledge-driven and model-based entrainment features across cohorts.

## Results

Individuals locally entrain with other humans in the group, but global entrainment and entrainment toward the digital agent remain limited and vary by cohort (adult vs. family groups).

## Applications

Informs the design of multi-party conversational agents (smart speakers, group assistants) about realistic expectations for how much users will entrain to them.
