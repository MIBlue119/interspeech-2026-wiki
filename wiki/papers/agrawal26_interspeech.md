# Amadea: An AI Companion for Pitch-Aware Spoken Language Practice

**TL;DR** — A demo system that gives learners of pitch-sensitive languages real-time visual feedback on how closely their pitch contour matches a native speaker's.

## Problem

Learners of pitch-accent languages (e.g., Japanese) or tonal languages (e.g., Mandarin) have few tools that give them direct, real-time feedback on their pitch production.

## Method

Amadea transcribes a learner's spoken input, generates a native-equivalent reference audio for the same phrase, aligns the two recordings, and compares their normalized F0 contours to produce a pitch-pattern score and a divergence graph, all within an AI companion interface that also supports open-ended dialogue.

## Results

As a system/demo paper, the abstract does not report quantitative accuracy figures; it describes the working pipeline for structured lessons and open-ended practice with real-time pitch feedback.
