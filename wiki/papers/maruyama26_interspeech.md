# Real-Time CARFAC/SAI-derived Pitchogram for Seeing and Correcting Pronunciation in Mandarin Chinese Tones

**TL;DR** — A pronunciation-training demo replaces conventional spectrograms with a real-time "pitchogram" that better reveals the fine temporal pitch structure learners need to master Mandarin tones.

## Problem

Conventional spectrogram-based visualization often fails to clearly show the temporal fine structure needed for learners to perceive and correct Mandarin Chinese tone production.

## Method

The system generates a real-time pitchogram derived from CARFAC (Cascade of Asymmetric Resonators with Fast-Acting Compression) and a Stabilised Auditory Image, displayed in a dual-screen interface alongside a reference pitchogram synthesized via Google Text-to-Speech, letting learners compare their own tone production to the reference.

## Results

As a demo paper, the abstract reports no formal accuracy evaluation; it describes the working real-time comparison interface for the four Mandarin tones.

## Applications

Computer-assisted pronunciation training (CAPT) tools for second-language learners of Mandarin Chinese and other tonal languages.
