# TMASC: Transmasculine Attitude and Speech Corpus

**TL;DR** — A new multimodal corpus of 196 transmasculine individuals combines vocal-health questionnaires with audio recordings to support research on transmasculine voice and speech.

## Problem

Research supporting transmasculine individuals' vocal health lacks a dedicated multimodal corpus combining self-reported attitudes with acoustic data.

## Method

The authors introduce TMASC, a corpus with questionnaire responses from 196 transmasculine individuals (including items on vocal health) and 66 audio recordings covering cough/throat-clearing samples, a reading passage, and session-specific questions, and describe the corpus's development and collection procedures.

## Results

Three case studies illustrate the corpus's utility: integrating perceptual and acoustic data, identifying group-level characteristics, and calibrating acoustic measurements; the paper reports no formal classification benchmark results.

## Applications

Research on transmasculine vocal health and voice characteristics, and development of more inclusive speech technology and clinical voice care.
