# Balancing Speech, Language and Hearing Science with Machine Learning Modeling in the Age of AI: "Know your Problem, Data, and Solution"

**TL;DR** — A keynote-style talk argues that speech and hearing science principles need to stay central alongside machine learning advances, drawing on decades of research into speaker variability, human perception, and large-scale conversational corpora.

## Problem

As machine learning and general AI drive major performance gains in speech technology, the talk argues that the underlying speech, language, and hearing science principles are increasingly under-emphasized in system development.

## Method

The talk surveys ways to balance speech/language/hearing science with ML modeling, covering speaker and speech variability (stress, emotion, vocal effort, Lombard effect, non-nativeness), human perception (e.g. cochlear implant innovations), and large-scale conversational language research such as team communications and historical archives.

## Results

As an invited talk rather than an empirical study, its "result" is a set of historical lessons and forward-looking guidance for combining domain science with AI-era speech modeling, aimed particularly at early-career researchers.
