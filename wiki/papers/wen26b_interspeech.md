# YUE-PUB-Speech: A Speech-based Pragmatic Understanding Benchmark for Cantonese

**TL;DR** — YUE-PUB-Speech is the first multimodal pragmatic understanding dataset for Cantonese, pairing pragmatically rich text with recorded speech; baseline experiments show adding speech signals improves pragmatic understanding compared to text-only models.

## Problem

Pragmatic understanding is essential for natural human communication, but nearly all existing research focuses on text-only settings, leaving spoken pragmatic understanding, especially for low-resource languages like Cantonese, unaddressed.

## Method

The authors construct YUE-PUB-Speech by sampling pragmatically rich Cantonese texts and recording corresponding speech with trained annotators, producing paired text-speech instances that capture pragmatic meaning without requiring fine-grained phonological or word-level alignment, then run baseline experiments comparing text-only and multimodal models.

## Results

Incorporating speech signals improves pragmatic understanding performance compared to text-only models, highlighting the value of multimodal resources for pragmatic research in speech and language processing.
