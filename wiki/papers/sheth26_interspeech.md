# Deriving Benchmarking Datasets from Long-Form Recordings: Challenges and Opportunities

**TL;DR** — A framework that standardizes 27 child-centered long-form recording datasets, provides a replicable benchmark pipeline, and embeds ethical governance into the ML workflow, demonstrated on a voice-type classification case study.

## Problem

Long-form child-centered recordings are valuable for studying early language development, but heterogeneous collection formats across sites, the absence of standardized benchmarks, and ML workflows that neglect privacy constraints for sensitive child speech all limit their use.

## Method

The authors build a three-part framework: a standardized collection of 27 child-centered long-form datasets built with open-source tools, a replicable pipeline for four speech-processing benchmarks, and ELSI, a role-based ecosystem that embeds ethical governance into the ML workflow, then demonstrate the combined framework on a voice-type classification case study.

## Results

The case study shows the three components (standardized data, replicable benchmarks, and governance) are mutually dependent for enabling responsible, generalizable benchmarking on long-form child speech recordings.
