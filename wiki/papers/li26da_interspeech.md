# Carrier-Aware Sound Zone Control for Parametric Array Loudspeakers

**TL;DR** — Treats the ultrasonic carrier signal, not just the audio sidebands, as a controllable variable in directional loudspeaker systems, boosting spatial audio contrast and efficiency.

## Problem

Parametric array loudspeakers create directional sound via ultrasonic carriers and nonlinear demodulation; prior sound-zone-control work optimizes only the sideband excitations while leaving the essential carrier unexplored as a control variable.

## Method

Proposes a carrier-aware sound zone control framework that treats the carrier as a new control dimension, using alternating optimization of sideband and carrier weights.

## Results

Simulations and experiments show the method outperforms sideband-only control, with broadband gains of 11.2 dB in acoustic contrast and 7.0 dB in target-zone SPL under identical power constraints.
