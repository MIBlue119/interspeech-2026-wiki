# SRF-SVB: Style-Consistent Singing Voice Beautifying via Rectified Flow

**TL;DR** — A rectified-flow model corrects pitch and rhythm of amateur singing while preserving the singer's own timbre and expressive style, outperforming prior beautification methods.

## Problem

Singing voice beautifying should correct pitch and rhythm while enhancing quality and preserving lyrics and the singer's timbre, but existing methods have limited generation quality/efficiency and often fail to preserve the singer's own style.

## Method

SRF-SVB is a style-consistent singing voice beautifying model based on rectified flow for high-fidelity, efficient pitch and rhythm correction, combined with a context-guided masked mel-spectrogram inpainting mechanism designed to preserve the amateur singer's unique timbre and expressive patterns.

## Results

On both English and Chinese test sets, SRF-SVB outperforms baseline models on most objective and subjective metrics.
