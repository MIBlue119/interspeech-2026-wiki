# VIP-MINGLE: A Corpus for Videoconference and In-Person Multimodal Interaction in Group Language Engagement

**TL;DR** — VIP-MINGLE is a 59-hour multimodal corpus of the same groups having conversations both in person and over videoconference, revealing that people behave differently enough across these settings to justify studying them together.

## Problem

Research on group conversation dynamics is usually siloed into either in-person or videoconferencing settings because few datasets capture the same groups in both, making it hard to compare behavior across settings.

## Method

The authors record 32 groups (105 participants) in paired within-subject sessions across in-person and videoconference settings, releasing raw audio/video, psychometric data, processed features such as diarized speech and facial expressions, and time-resolved human annotations.

## Results

Analysis reveals significant behavioral shifts across multiple modalities between in-person and videoconference settings, confirming the need for a corpus that bridges both.
