# Improved modeling of vocal fold contacting and de-contacting in a geometric vocal fold model

**TL;DR** — A biomechanically-motivated geometric vocal fold model with a more gradual opening/closing during partial contact, plus glottal-area-waveform skewing, produces synthetic speech preferred over a prior model in 58.1% of A/B comparisons with higher mean opinion scores.

## Problem

Existing geometric vocal fold models for articulatory speech synthesis don't fully capture the gradual, biomechanically realistic opening and closing behavior of the vocal folds during phases of partial glottal contact, limiting naturalness.

## Method

The authors propose a biomechanically-motivated mechanism for a more gradual opening and closing of the glottis during partial vocal-fold contact, combined with a mechanism to skew the glottal area waveform left or right, implemented in an articulatory speech synthesizer and compared against a similar model lacking the improved contacting mechanisms via a perception experiment across a range of pre-phonatory settings.

## Results

In an A/B comparison, sentences synthesized with the new model were preferred over the previous model in 58.1% of comparisons, with mean opinion scores of 3.02 versus 2.68 for the new and previous models respectively.
