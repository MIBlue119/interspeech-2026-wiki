# The First Environmental Sound Deepfake Detection Challenge: Benchmarking Robustness, Evaluation, and Insights

**TL;DR** — Reports on the first-ever shared task for detecting fake environmental sounds (alarms, gunshots, crowds), which drew 97 teams and nearly 1,750 submissions, and distills what made top systems work.

## Problem

Audio generation can now create highly realistic fake environmental soundscapes that could be misused for deceptive content like fake alarms or gunshots, but unlike speech/singing deepfake detection, environmental sound deepfake detection (ESDD) remains underexplored.

## Method

Launches the first ESDD challenge, describing task formulation, dataset construction, evaluation protocols, and baseline systems, then analyzes architectural choices and training strategies common among top-performing submitted systems.

## Results

The challenge attracted 97 registered teams and 1,748 valid submissions, yielding empirical insight into which architectural and training choices work best for environmental sound deepfake detection.

## Applications
