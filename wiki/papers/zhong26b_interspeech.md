# A Benchmark for Early-stage Parkinson's Disease Detection from Speech

**TL;DR** — The first standardized, speaker-independent benchmark for detecting early-stage Parkinson's disease from speech, designed to make previously incomparable studies finally comparable across datasets and methods.

## Problem

Early-stage Parkinson's disease detection from speech is clinically meaningful but underexplored, and published results are hard to compare because studies differ in datasets, languages, tasks, evaluation protocols, and definitions of "early-stage."

## Method

Proposes the first benchmark for speech-based early-stage PD detection with a speaker-independent split for fair, replicable cross-method evaluation on researcher-accessible datasets, covering three common speech tasks and different training-resource settings, with multi-dimensional evaluation breakdowns by dataset, aggregation level, gender, and disease stage.

## Results

Provides a replicable reference and actionable insights across evaluated methods, laying groundwork for robust, clinically meaningful, and fairly comparable speech-based early PD detection research.
