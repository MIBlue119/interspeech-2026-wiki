# Benchmarking Speech Systems for Frontline Health Conversations: The DISPLACE-M Challenge

**TL;DR** — The DISPLACE-M challenge introduces a benchmark of real, noisy, multi-speaker medical conversations between frontline health workers and patients, with baseline systems for diarization, ASR, topic ID, and summarization.

## Problem

Goal-oriented, real-world medical dialogues involve spontaneous, noisy, overlapping multi-speaker speech that existing conversational AI benchmarks don't adequately represent.

## Method

The challenge releases 40 hours of development and 15 hours of blind evaluation recordings of frontline health worker conversations, along with baseline systems across four tasks — speaker diarization, ASR, topic identification, and dialogue summarization — evaluated with DER, tcpWER, and ROUGE-L.

## Results

The paper reports the Phase-I evaluation setup, data, tasks, and baseline system results, establishing a shared benchmark for this domain rather than a single new best result.
