# AfriVox-v2: A Domain-Verticalized Benchmark for In-the-Wild African Speech Recognition

**TL;DR** — A realistic, sector-by-sector benchmark of unscripted African-language speech that exposes how far current speech models, including newer large multimodal ones, still fall short in noisy real-world deployment conditions.

## Problem

African languages remain dramatically underrepresented in speech benchmarks, and even the benchmarks that do include them typically use clean, scripted audio and lack fine-grained evaluation across real-world application domains.

## Method

AfriVox-v2 introduces "in the wild" unscripted audio across its supported languages with strict domain verticalization, evaluating accuracy across ten sectors (government, finance, health, agriculture, and others) plus targeted tests on numbers and named entities, and benchmarks a new generation of models including Sahara-v2, Gemini 3 Flash, and Omnilingual CTC.

## Results

The benchmark reveals a substantial generalization gap: modern speech models perform markedly worse in specialized, noisy African deployment contexts than on cleaner prior benchmarks.

## Applications

A reference blueprint for developers building localized, domain-specific voice AI for African languages, and a diagnostic benchmark for teams choosing or fine-tuning ASR models for low-resource, noisy deployment settings.
