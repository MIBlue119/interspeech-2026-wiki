# UG-Bench: A Comprehensive Benchmark for Evaluating Large Audio-Language Models

**TL;DR** — A unified benchmark testing speech perception, audio perception, speech generation, and spoken language understanding together (not separately) finds significant gaps in instruction following and generation quality across 11 LALMs and 5 speech-generation models.

## Problem

Evaluating Large Audio-Language Models (LALMs) is hard because of the diversity of speech and audio tasks, and existing benchmarks lack a unified framework, typically focusing on either understanding or generation but not both.

## Method

The authors introduce UG-Bench, a comprehensive benchmark systematically assessing LALMs across four competencies — speech perception, audio perception, speech generation, and spoken language understanding — with a decoupled design that enables standardized comparisons and supports new tasks.

## Results

Evaluating 11 LALMs and 5 specialized speech generation models reveals significant gaps in instruction following and generation quality across the field, underscoring the need for better semantic understanding.
