# Before the Turn: Investigating Motion Cues Preceding Speech in Dyadic Interaction

**TL;DR** — A systematic analysis of body-motion cues that precede speech in two-person conversation, showing upper-body movement can predict an upcoming turn up to three seconds in advance.

## Problem

Turn-taking prediction models are typically reactive and lag because they ignore the asynchronous pre-speech motion onsets that occur across different body parts before someone actually speaks.

## Method

Evaluates a Transformer on InterAct 3D skeletal motion data, varying observation windows and lead times across diverse turn-claiming and floor-holding scenarios.

## Results

Upper-body signals support stable predictions up to 3.0s before speech; turn-claiming shows a 1.5s gradual preparatory build-up while floor-holding shows a more explosive burst of motion.
