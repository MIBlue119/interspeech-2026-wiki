# VoxKit: Desktop Phone Alignment and Goodness of Pronunciation Analysis

**TL;DR** — A desktop application that unifies forced alignment and goodness-of-pronunciation scoring in one pipeline, aimed at speech researchers who work with clinical populations.

## Problem

No existing desktop tool combines forced phone alignment with goodness-of-pronunciation analysis in a single interface, forcing clinical speech researchers to stitch together separate tools.

## Method

VoxKit lets researchers register datasets, choose and train alignment engines, generate and compare alignments, and compute phone-level goodness-of-pronunciation scores end to end within one workbench.

## Results

The paper presents VoxKit as a working, extensible tool rather than reporting benchmark accuracy numbers.

None released (as of this page's `updated` date). If you are an author with a repo, please claim this entry — see CONTRIBUTING.md.

## Applications

Clinical and developmental speech research, e.g. scoring child speech-sound disorders or tracking pronunciation therapy progress.
