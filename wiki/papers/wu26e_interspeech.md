# CrossPhon-Tonal: Streamlining Cross-language Modeling for Forced Alignment in Low-resource Tonal Languages

**TL;DR** — An automated cross-language forced-alignment approach for low-resource tonal languages that encodes tone mappings automatically, matching hand-crafted expert mappings without needing scarce phonetic expertise.

## Problem

Forced alignment needs language-specific acoustic models that most tonal languages lack, and existing cross-language modeling solutions either ignore lexical tone entirely or require scarce phonetic expertise to hand-craft tone mappings between languages.

## Method

CrossPhon-Tonal introduces a novel tone encoding that automates tone mappings within the cross-language modeling pipeline, tested on six typologically diverse tonal languages.

## Results

Performs comparably to human expert-generated mappings for cross-language tonal forced alignment, and matches or outperforms the state-of-the-art Global English cross-language model.
