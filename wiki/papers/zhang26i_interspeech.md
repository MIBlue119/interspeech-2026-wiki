# Tense Voice, Not Falsetto: An F0-specific Physiological Byproduct of Extreme High-Pitch Tone in Kaihui Xiang

**TL;DR** — Acoustic analysis of Kaihui Xiang Chinese shows its extreme high-pitch tone, long assumed to be falsetto, is actually produced with tense modal voice, and this tenseness appears to be a physiological byproduct of high F0 rather than an independent phonological feature.

## Problem

The extreme high-pitch tone (T4) in Xiang Chinese has traditionally been labeled falsetto, but whether this characterization is acoustically accurate, and whether the voice quality is an active phonological choice or just a physiological pitch byproduct, was unresolved.

## Method

The authors conduct a multidimensional acoustic analysis across the entire tonal inventory of Kaihui Xiang, examining spectral tilt and other voice-quality measures to compare T4 against both other tones and the acoustic signature of falsetto.

## Results

T4 shows a flattened lower-frequency spectral tilt characteristic of tense modal voice rather than the steep tilt and turbulent noise typical of falsetto, and T4 becomes acoustically indistinguishable from tone T6 wherever their F0 levels converge, indicating the tenseness is an F0-driven physiological byproduct rather than an independent phonological feature.
