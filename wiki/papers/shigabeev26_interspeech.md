# Dialogs: a studio-quality expressive conversational Russian speech corpus for dialog assistants

**TL;DR** — A new 20.6-hour studio-quality Russian conversational speech corpus with per-utterance emotion labels rates higher on expressiveness and naturalness than existing Russian read-speech resources.

## Problem

Existing Russian speech resources are mostly read-speech, lacking the turn-taking rhythm and expressive prosody needed to train natural-sounding conversational dialog assistants.

## Method

The authors introduce "Dialogs," a studio-quality Russian conversational speech corpus of 20.6 hours of face-to-face acted dialogs (44.1 kHz stereo, 11,796 utterances, 3 speakers), providing per-utterance style/emotion labels across 12 categories, and validate quality with crowd MOS tests and by training a VITS2 model as proof of concept.

## Results

Crowd MOS tests show audio quality and intelligibility comparable to strong Russian studio baselines while rating higher for expressiveness and conversational naturalness; the VITS2 proof-of-concept demonstrates the corpus supports training expressive, dialog-like TTS despite limited per-speaker data.
