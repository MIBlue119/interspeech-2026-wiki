# BeatGain - A Rhythmic Pattern Enhancement Algorithm for Music Listening with Cochlear Implants

**TL;DR** — Amplifying beat-aligned percussive hits and attenuating off-beat ones makes music sound rhythmically clearer for cochlear implant users, and listeners significantly preferred it over existing remixing approaches.

## Problem

Cochlear implants (CIs) restore speech understanding but music perception often remains limited, and CI users typically prefer music with a clear beat and reduced instrumental complexity, which existing enhancement strategies address mainly by boosting vocals or percussion generically.

## Method

The authors propose BeatGain, a music-mixing approach that explicitly adapts to a track's rhythmic structure: percussive events aligned with the main beat are amplified while weaker off-beat events are attenuated.

## Results

Objective measures show reduced complexity versus both the unprocessed signal and a baseline remixing approach, and in a listening experiment BeatGain was significantly preferred over reference conditions for rhythmic clarity, and in nearly all comparisons for overall quality.
