# NewAppVoice: Tools for Visualizing and Correcting Acoustic Measures

**TL;DR** — NewAppVoice is an open-access, reproducible workflow that combines multiple formant-extraction algorithms with synchronized visualization so researchers can efficiently spot-check and correct automatic acoustic measurements for under-documented languages.

## Problem

Phonetic analysis of little-studied languages, especially those with complex or rare segmental inventories, often lacks reference formant values, so automatic extraction produces systematic errors that require extensive manual verification, and most large-scale scripts offer no interactive feedback for this.

## Method

The authors build NewAppVoice, a reproducible workflow that integrates multiple formant-calculation algorithms with synchronized visualization of signals and measurements, combining automated processing with structured manual verification rather than a fully automated pipeline.

## Results

The paper presents the tool and workflow rather than a benchmark result, releasing the software and derived applications as freely available open-access tools.

## Applications

Aimed at phoneticians and linguists documenting under-resourced languages who need transparent, reproducible acoustic measurement with human-in-the-loop correction.
