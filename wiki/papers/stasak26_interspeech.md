# CalliOpeNLP: A Standalone Digital Health Voice Data Collection Research Tool

**TL;DR** — A free, open-source, standalone tool that automates clinical voice-health data collection across 18 validated voice tasks, avoiding the insecure data transfer and compliance headaches of manual or commercial alternatives.

## Problem

Manual clinical voice data collection faces compliance, annotation-standard, security, interoperability, and in-person interaction-bias challenges, and existing automated solutions are commercial, fee-based, and may require insecure data transfer.

## Method

CalliOpeNLP is a Python-based open-source tool that gives computer-generated spoken instructions for 18 clinically validated voice tasks, then records, labels, and tracks patient compliance using low-latency voice-to-text transcript analytics, all standalone without external data transfer.

## Results

Presented as a working research tool rather than a benchmark study; the paper describes its capabilities as a practical enhancement to automated voice data collection.

## Code

## Applications

Clinical voice-health biobank data collection for researchers studying voice disorders, without dependence on paid commercial software or insecure cloud transfer.
