# Montreal Forced Aligner and the state of speech-to-text alignment in 2026

**TL;DR** — A decade-later update on the widely used Montreal Forced Aligner (MFA 3.0), documenting new language coverage, harmonized IPA dictionaries, and cross-language adaptation, and benchmarking it against classic and neural aligners.

## Problem

Forced alignment tools need to keep pace with expanding language coverage, harmonized phonetic dictionaries, and newer neural aligners, and MFA's development since its 2016 release needed documentation and benchmarking.

## Method

Documents MFA 3.0's developments since version 1.0 (expanded language/dialect coverage, harmonized IPA dictionaries, model adaptation, cross-language phone remapping) and evaluates its performance on English, Japanese, and Korean against classic and neural forced aligners.

## Results

MFA 3.0 achieves state-of-the-art or near state-of-the-art performance across all four benchmark datasets with mean boundary errors below 15 ms, and adaptation/cross-language remapping is effective for out-of-distribution languages.
