# Working Together on Technologies: A Case Study of Collaboration in Aotearoa

[PDF](https://www.isca-archive.org/interspeech_2026/hutchinson26_interspeech.pdf) · [ISCA page](https://www.isca-archive.org/interspeech_2026/hutchinson26_interspeech.html) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.21437/Interspeech.2026-1573)

**TL;DR** — This paper presents a collaborative case study between an Indigenous Māori organization and a global tech company to co-develop a text-to-speech system supporting New Zealand English and te reo Māori place names.

## Problem

Global text-to-speech technologies historically marginalize Indigenous languages like te reo Māori by focusing primarily on major global languages and treating language as decontextualized data. This lack of localization creates friction and replicates colonial harms, highlighting the need for equitable partnership models that respect Indigenous data sovereignty and cultural values.

## Method

The partnership co-developed an Engagement Framework based on Māori concepts of manaakitanga, whakawhanaungatanga, and taunaki to guide collaboration and resolve divergent expectations. Technical and procedural adaptations included rewriting recording scripts to reflect geographical and cultural reality, selecting voice talent with community oversight, and expanding the pronunciation lexicon to handle orthographic variations like missing macrons or doubled vowels. The resulting Text-to-Speech system integrates a New Zealand English accent with accurate pronunciation of te reo Māori place names for driving guidance applications.

## Results

The evaluation combined quantitative assessments by New Zealand English-speaking raters on driving direction guidance with a qualitative mixed-methods evaluation session involving five representatives from each partner organization. Evaluators tested the system using a co-developed list of diverse te reo Māori place names to ensure authentic pronunciation standards. The collaboration successfully yielded a deployed New Zealand TTS voice and improved keyboard support for macron diacritics.
