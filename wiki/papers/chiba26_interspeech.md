# Speech-based Psychological Crisis Assessment using LLMs

[PDF](https://www.isca-archive.org/interspeech_2026/chiba26_interspeech.pdf) · [ISCA page](https://www.isca-archive.org/interspeech_2026/chiba26_interspeech.html) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.21437/Interspeech.2026-997)

**TL;DR** — An LLM-based pipeline converts acoustic paralinguistic cues into explicit text annotations via speech models to assist in automated three-way psychological crisis assessment, achieving a macro F1 score of 0.802 on authentic hotline speech.

## Problem

Manual psychological crisis assessment on support hotlines suffers from subjectivity and staffing constraints, while automated systems either rely solely on text transcripts that miss critical vocal affect or use end-to-end speech models poorly suited for data-scarce clinical domains. This gap prevents reliable, consistent triage in privacy-sensitive and resource-limited emergency environments.

## Method

The framework processes raw hotline audio using Paraformer-zh for ASR transcription and Step-Audio-R1 to extract non-verbal paralinguistic cues (such as sobbing, low volume, or trembling voice) guided by triage assessment forms (TAF). These paralinguistic descriptions are injected into the transcript, which is then fed into a Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct model fine-tuned via LoRA. To regularise training, the model uses reasoning-enhanced training with auxiliary TAF-conditioned diagnostic rationales generated by gpt-oss-120b. Additionally, chunk-based data augmentation divides 154 long calls into 900+ five-minute chunks, aggregated at test time via majority voting.

## Results

Evaluated on a dataset of 154 authentic Chinese psychological support hotline calls (approx. 100 hours) using 5-fold cross-validation, the system achieves a macro F1-score of 0.802 and an accuracy of 0.805. It outperforms an OpenSMILE eGeMAPSv02 acoustic baseline (macro F1: 0.471, accuracy: 0.564), a zero-shot gpt-oss-120b LLM baseline with enriched transcripts (macro F1: 0.371, accuracy: 0.455), and a Qwen2.5-Omni-7B SpeechLLM end-to-end baseline (macro F1: 0.551, accuracy: 0.586).

## Applications

Clinicians and psychological support hotline operators can deploy this system as decision-support software to triage mental health emergency calls more consistently under human oversight.

## Limitations

The dataset is restricted to 154 calls from a single Chinese hotline center under strict privacy guidelines, and auxiliary reasoning targets are LLM-generated rather than backed by domain-expert rationales for intermediate clinical domains.
