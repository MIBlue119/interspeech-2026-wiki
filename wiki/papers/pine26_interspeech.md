# Two Lessons Learned from the SGILE Project: Efficient Building and Evaluation of TTS Voices

**TL;DR** — A Show & Tell demo combining an open-source low-resource TTS toolkit with a listening-effort-minimizing evaluation method, built from work on Indigenous language education speech technology.

## Problem

Building and evaluating TTS voices for under-resourced languages, such as Indigenous languages, is hampered by both limited audio data for training and the difficulty of gathering reliable listener evaluation when few listeners are available.

## Method

The authors present two contributions from the "Speech Generation for Indigenous Language Education" (SGILE) project: the open-source EveryVoice TTS Toolkit, designed to build high-quality voices from the constrained audio amounts typical of under-resourced languages, and a methodical evaluation of speech evaluation techniques aimed at maximizing insight while minimizing listener effort; both are combined in an interactive web-app demo.

## Results

As a Show & Tell demo paper, it does not report benchmark accuracy numbers; it presents the EveryVoice toolkit and a listening-test design as practical tools, demonstrated live via the web app.

## Applications

Building and evaluating TTS voices for under-resourced and Indigenous languages with limited data and limited listener availability.
