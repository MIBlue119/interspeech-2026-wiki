# Sound Reactor Mission: Gamified Misophonia Assessment to Bridge the Gap in Audiology and Hearing Care

**TL;DR** — A six-minute browser game assesses misophonia by measuring speech intelligibility against a personally annoying masking sound, revealing much lower intelligibility than with standard test noise.

## Problem

Misophonia causes strong emotional and physiological reactions to specific everyday sounds, but audiology currently lacks a standardized, scalable way to assess it.

## Method

Sound Reactor Mission (SRM) is a browser-based, gamified speech-in-noise assessment requiring only headphones: in a six-minute "space mission," participants complete adaptive Speech Reception Threshold estimation, select a personally annoying masker, compare speech intelligibility across three masker conditions, and complete an Acceptable Noise Level measure.

## Results

Pilot data (N=14, 100% completion, mean time 6.5 min) showed lower speech intelligibility for the participant's own selected annoying sound (59.2%) than for synthetic (71.7%) or standard masking noise (86.3%).
