# Tongue-Shape Strategies for Standard Mandarin Retroflex Sibilants: A Preliminary Ultrasound and Unsupervised Clustering Study

**TL;DR** — Ultrasound imaging and unsupervised clustering of tongue contours reveal that Standard Mandarin retroflex sibilants are produced with several distinct, speaker-specific tongue-shape strategies (domed, humped, concave), not a single canonical retroflex shape.

## Problem

Standard Mandarin retroflex sibilants are often described as non-prototypical retroflexes with diverse articulatory realizations that aren't well characterized by existing descriptive frameworks.

## Method

The authors use tongue ultrasound imaging on five female native speakers from diverse regional backgrounds producing alveolar-retroflex sibilant minimal pairs in carrier sentences, extract 42-point tongue contours aligned by centroid, and apply unsupervised clustering of shape descriptors.

## Results

Clustering yields six contour clusters consolidated into three super-strategies (domed, humped, concave) that differ from prior descriptive frameworks, with compositional analysis revealing systematic, stable between-speaker preferences.
