# Automated Measurement of Geniohyoid Muscle Thickness During Speech Using Deep Learning and Ultrasound

**TL;DR** — SMMA automates the previously manual, time-consuming measurement of geniohyoid muscle thickness from speech ultrasound with near-human accuracy, revealing systematic differences in muscle activation across vowels and sexes.

## Problem

Manually measuring muscle morphology from ultrasound during speech is time-consuming and limits the scale of studies into speech motor control and swallowing disorders.

## Method

SMMA is a fully automated framework combining deep learning segmentation with skeleton-based thickness quantification to analyze geniohyoid muscle dynamics from ultrasound video.

## Results

Validation shows near-human-level accuracy (Dice: 0.9037, MAE: 0.53mm, r=0.901); applied to Cantonese vowel production (N=11), /a:/ shows significantly greater geniohyoid thickness (7.29mm) than /i:/ (5.95mm, p<0.001, large effect size), consistent with its role in mandibular depression, and males show 5-8% greater thickness reflecting anatomical scaling.
