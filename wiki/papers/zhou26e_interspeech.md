# Beyond One-Size-Fits-All: Personalized and Culturally Adaptive Emotional TTS via Interactive Optimization of Individual Emotion Perception Spaces

**TL;DR** — An interactive genetic algorithm personalizes each listener's arousal-valence emotion space, producing emotional TTS that Japanese, Chinese, and Indonesian participants perceive as more accurately emotional than one-size-fits-all models.

## Problem

Emotional TTS systems mostly rely on discrete emotion labels or averaged dimensional (arousal-valence) representations, but emotional perception varies across individuals and cultures, causing mismatches between the modeled and perceived emotion.

## Method

The authors propose a personalized, culturally adaptive emotional TTS framework that performs interactive optimization of each listener's individualized arousal-valence perception space using an Interactive Genetic Algorithm, adapting emotion representations per listener rather than using averaged values.

## Results

Evaluations with Japanese, Chinese, and Indonesian participants show the personalized approach produces speech with more perceptually aligned emotional expression than models using averaged arousal-valence values, highlighting the value of personalization and cultural adaptation.
