# Age-related Differences in Acoustic Realization of Aspirated Fricatives in Shaxi Bai

**TL;DR** — Comparing three age groups of Shaxi Bai speakers finds younger speakers show a different pattern of covarying acoustic cues for aspirated fricatives, suggesting the aspiration contrast is being actively reorganized across generations.

## Problem

Aspirated fricatives are realized through multiple interacting acoustic parameters rather than one defining cue, and it was unclear how these parameters vary across speaker age groups in Shaxi Bai, an understudied language variety.

## Method

The authors analyze acoustic data from 30 Shaxi Bai speakers across three age groups, focusing on three parameters — initial duration ratio, spectral center of gravity, and H1*-H2* — using linear mixed-effects models.

## Results

The relative magnitude of acoustic effects varies across fricative types and age groups; temporal and spectral measures show robust differences while the phonatory measure is weaker and less consistent; young speakers show a distinct covariation pattern among acoustic parameters, consistent with an ongoing reorganization of the aspiration contrast across generations.
