# High-Quality Speech Synthesis for Under-Resourced Ethiopian Languages

**TL;DR** — Builds high-quality TTS for Amharic and Afan Oromo by recording 200 hours of studio speech and fine-tuning SpeechT5, reaching human MOS ratings of 4.65 and 4.43 respectively.

## Problem

Amharic and Afan Oromo are widely spoken but under-resourced languages in Ethiopia, lacking high-quality TTS systems, and Amharic's linguistic complexities (gemination, context-sensitive pronunciation) add further difficulty.

## Method

Creates a phonetically rich text corpus and professionally records 200 hours of studio speech (100 hours per language), plus a special extended dataset for Amharic's gemination and pronunciation complexities, then fine-tunes the Transformer-based, self-supervised SpeechT5 model.

## Results

Human raters give mean opinion scores of 4.65 for Amharic and 4.43 for Afan Oromo, indicating high naturalness, intelligibility, and pronunciation accuracy.
