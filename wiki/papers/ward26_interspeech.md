# The Interspeech 2026 Challenge on Transfer of Pragmatic Intent in Speech-to-Speech Translation

**TL;DR** — A new Interspeech 2026 shared task on preserving pragmatic intent (not just words) in speech-to-speech translation, reporting that even the best submitted system still trails human performance by 1.2 points on a 5-point scale.

## Problem

Current speech-to-speech translation systems do not prioritize pragmatic fidelity, meaning tone, intent, and nuance can be lost even when words are translated correctly, hurting cross-language conversation support.

## Method

Describes the design, datasets, and evaluation methods for the Interspeech 2026 Transfer of Pragmatic Intent challenge and reports the results across submitted systems.

## Results

The best-performing system still lags human performance by 1.2 points on a 5-point scale, and the paper notes both strengths and weaknesses of the challenge's own evaluation methods.
