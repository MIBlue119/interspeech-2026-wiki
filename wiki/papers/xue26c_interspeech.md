# NVV-SuperBench: Beyond Words, Beyond Quality—Benchmarking Nonverbal Vocalizations in Speech Generation

**TL;DR** — A bilingual benchmark with a 45-type taxonomy that, for the first time, jointly evaluates whether speech-generation systems produce the right nonverbal vocalizations (laughs, sighs, sobs), place them correctly, and keep them perceptually salient.

## Problem

Nonverbal vocalizations like laughing, sighing, and sobbing are essential for human-like speech, but standardized evaluation rarely jointly assesses whether systems generate the intended NVVs, place them correctly, and keep them salient without harming overall speech quality.

## Method

Introduces NVV-SuperBench, a bilingual English/Chinese benchmark with a unified 45-type taxonomy and a multi-axis protocol evaluating NVV-specific controllability, placement, and perceptual salience, benchmarking 15 speech generation systems across prompt-based and tag-based control paradigms using objective metrics, human listening tests, and LLM-based multi-rater evaluation.

## Results

NVV controllability often decouples from overall speech quality, and low-SNR oral cues plus long-duration affective NVVs remain persistent bottlenecks across current systems.

## Applications

Benchmarking and improving expressive TTS/voice-generation systems that need to produce believable laughs, sighs, and other nonverbal vocal cues.
