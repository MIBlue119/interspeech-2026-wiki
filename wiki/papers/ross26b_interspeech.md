# Sexualised Synthetic Personas Encode and Amplify Gendered Power Asymmetries through Voice

**TL;DR** — A listening study of a popular commercial voice-AI platform's sexualised AI voices finds they reproduce narrow, largely binary and heteronormative gender performances, with female-coded voices described as sexualized and submissive and male-coded voices as dominant.

## Problem

New voice AI technologies could enable sexual empowerment and diverse gender expression, but toxic masculinity, heteronormativity, and abuse toward women and LGBTQ+ people remain pervasive, and how commercial synthetic voices reproduce gendered power dynamics was underexamined.

## Method

Drawing on a Feminist HCI perspective, the authors conduct a listening experiment where a diverse group of participants evaluate male- and female-coded synthetic voices presented with either sexualised scripts or neutral text, combining quantitative adjective selection, qualitative free-text responses, and acoustic analysis.

## Results

Results reveal a narrow range of gender expression that is largely binary and heteronormative; female-coded voices are more often described using sexualised and submissive terms, while male-coded voices are more often associated with dominance and positive traits.
