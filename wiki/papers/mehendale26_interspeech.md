# Indic DiarBench: A Multilingual Joint Diarization and ASR Benchmark for Indian Languages

**TL;DR** — Indic DiarBench is a new joint diarization-and-ASR benchmark spanning all 22 scheduled Indian languages, built from natural multi-speaker conversations, revealing gaps in current commercial and multimodal systems on Indian speech.

## Problem

There has been no unified, natural, multi-speaker benchmark covering joint speaker diarization and ASR across all of India's scheduled languages, despite conversational features like code-mixing, dialectal variation, and frequent overlap that make Indian speech especially challenging.

## Method

The authors release roughly 108 hours of human-corrected, time-aligned, speaker-attributed transcriptions from near-field meetings, far-field recordings, and in-the-wild audio across all 22 scheduled Indian languages, and evaluate leading commercial speech APIs and multimodal LLMs as baselines.

## Results

The benchmark establishes baseline joint ASR-and-diarization performance for current systems on Indian speech and is released as an open-access resource.
