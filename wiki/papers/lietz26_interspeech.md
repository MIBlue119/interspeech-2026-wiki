# Making Room for Speech Diversity: A 50 Year Retrospective of Speech Science and Technology through a Neurodivergent Lens

**TL;DR** — A 50-year scoping review of Interspeech and ICASSP papers on neurodivergent speech finds recurring medicalized framing, little involvement of neurodivergent people in research, and ableist language, and proposes ways forward.

## Problem

Despite growing interest in diversity and inclusion in speech technology research, it was unclear how disability and neurodivergence have actually been framed across decades of Interspeech-adjacent research discourse.

## Method

The authors conduct a scoping content and discourse analysis of relevant Interspeech and ICASSP papers on neurodivergent speech published between 1976 and 2025.

## Results

The review identifies three recurring issues: medicalized/interventionist framing that treats neurodivergent traits as deficits, minimal involvement of neurodivergent individuals in the research process, and ableist language contributing to marginalization.
