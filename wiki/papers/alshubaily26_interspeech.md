# The SSPNet Speaker Personality Corpus Version 2: Investigating the Role of Language Understanding in Automatic Personality Perception

**TL;DR** — An upgraded release of the SSPNet Personality Corpus adds full transcripts, a finer 100-point rating scale, and both language-aware and language-blind rater groups, opening up new multimodal personality-perception research.

## Problem

The original SSPNet corpus for automatic personality perception was audio-only, used a coarse 9-point rating scale, and couldn't support studies of how understanding the spoken language affects personality judgments.

## Method

The authors extend the corpus with full transcriptions, re-annotate personality on a 100-point scale, and collect ratings from two rater groups distinguished by whether they understand the recordings' language, releasing reproducible unimodal and multimodal baselines.

## Results

The release establishes working unimodal and multimodal baselines on the new corpus, demonstrating it supports regression-style personality prediction that the original 9-point corpus could not.
