# The ArtComp dataset: Articulatory and Acoustic Measurements of Swedish in Speech with Naturally Manipulated Jaw Position

**TL;DR** — ArtComp is a new time-aligned articulatory and acoustic dataset of Swedish speech where jaw position is naturally perturbed via varying vocal effort, from soft to shouting, enabling study of compensatory articulator behavior.

## Problem

Research on adaptive articulatory behavior benefits from data where jaw position is perturbed by natural (rather than artificial) means, but such datasets are scarce, especially outside major languages.

## Method

The authors collect time-aligned electromagnetic articulography (EMA), electroglottography (EGG), video, and acoustic recordings from seven native Central Standard Swedish speakers producing speech across a range of vocal effort from soft to shouting, and describe the collection and post-processing methodology in detail.

## Results

The dataset provides variable and exaggerated jaw opening data with a preview of time-variation and 2D sensor tracings illustrating the processed articulatory signals.

## Applications

A resource for phoneticians and speech scientists studying how articulators compensate for perturbed jaw position, and for training or evaluating articulatory speech synthesis models.
