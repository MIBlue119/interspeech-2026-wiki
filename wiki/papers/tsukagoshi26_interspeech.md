# Distilling Structured Reasoning into SpeechLLMs for Spoken Language Understanding

**TL;DR** — Training a SpeechLLM to imitate a text-LLM's full reasoning trace — including why wrong intent candidates get rejected — sharpens its decision boundaries between similar intents, with zero extra cost at inference since only the final label is used.

## Problem

Speech LLMs (SpeechLLMs) are promising for end-to-end spoken language understanding, but conventional fine-tuning for tasks like intent classification struggles to separate semantically adjacent intent categories.

## Method

The authors propose Reasoning-Guided Fine-Tuning (RG-FT), which distills structured reasoning trajectories generated by DeepSeek-R1 — candidate enumeration, rejection reasoning, and label prediction — into SpeechLLMs as a multitask objective, forcing the model to explicitly verbalize why incorrect candidates are rejected during training while only using direct label prediction at inference.

## Results

Across multiple SLU benchmarks and six SpeechLLMs, RG-FT consistently improves SLU performance, with zero additional computational cost at inference time.
