# Towards Robust Ultrasound-based Silent Speech Recognition Learning Physics-Aware and Context-Rich Representations

**TL;DR** — A framework for decoding speech from tongue ultrasound video that explicitly models the physics of imaging artifacts and preserves temporal context, improving robustness of ultrasound-based silent speech recognition.

## Problem

Silent Speech Recognition from Ultrasound Tongue Imaging is challenging because imaging artifacts and complex temporal dependencies make it hard to extract robust features from the ultrasound sequences.

## Method

Uses a temporal clustering strategy to downsample sequences while preserving critical context, a physics-aware augmentation that enforces robustness against imaging perturbations, and temporal masking with a hybrid 3D-convolutional plus bidirectional-recurrent architecture to promote spatiotemporal representation learning.

## Results

Significantly outperforms baselines, validating that modeling physical consistency and structural completeness is essential for robust ultrasound-based silent speech recognition.
