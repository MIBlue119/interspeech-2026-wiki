# Modulation of Phonetic Realizations in Cantonese Dialogue with Human and AI Interlocutors

**TL;DR** — A Wizard-of-Oz study finding that Cantonese speakers subtly change their pronunciation, such as shorter words and shifted tones, when they believe they are talking to an AI rather than a human.

## Problem

Prior human-AI speech accommodation research focused on intonation languages and emotionally expressive systems, leaving it unclear how interlocutor identity affects fine-grained phonetic detail in tone languages like Cantonese.

## Method

Collected scripted dialogues in neutral and negative emotional contexts with human versus AI interlocutors, using a Wizard-of-Oz setup, from 17 native Cantonese speakers, measuring word duration, tone F0, and vowel formants.

## Results

Speaking to an apparent AI shortened target-word duration and shifted specific tones relative to speaking to a human, while the emotional-context effect was only marginal.
